# Changelog

## [1.0.0]

- First release: codices for books, videos, articles, podcasts, lectures and courses, with a preface and questions to the source.
- Pages in your own words, laid out the Cornell way: questions on the margin, the golden line, the colophon. Pasted text goes aside as the author's words. Speaking aloud through the phone's recogniser.
- Ink that fades along each page's forgetting curve (FSRS-4.5), and restorations that take turns: from memory, the margin questions, the lacunae. Missed words show in cinnabar.
- Candles staked before looking, the book of errors for confident mistakes, the scribe's eye for calibration.
- The novice who asks for plain explanations; examples, parables, disputes and the seal of practice on the margins.
- Illumination earned page by page: initial, rubrics, vines, gold leaf, seal.
- Matins and Compline bells, closing a codex in one sentence and three words, the library, the chronicle, backup to one JSON file.
- English and Russian.
